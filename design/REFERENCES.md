## Skills synthesized

- frontend-design: subject-specific identity, one memorable composition, exact copy.
- design-taste-frontend: brief inference, audit, type/grid discipline, mobile QA.
- redesign-existing-projects: retain Next.js, preserve working sample and intake.
- image-to-code and imagegen-frontend-web: analyze separate sections deeply; user
  overrides generated references, so inspected real captures are the source.
- brandkit: one identity concept carried across type, document, mark and UI.
- minimalist-ui: paper surfaces, open layouts, sparse functional rules.
- industrial-brutalist-ui: precise catalog grid and tabular values only; tactical
  chrome, fake telemetry and decorative numbering do not fit this service.
- high-end-visual-design: calibrated hierarchy, purposeful depth and tactile states.
- gpt-taste: narrative sequence, broad headlines, varied layouts and coordinated
  motion. The user's follow-up expressly requests rich reference-informed animation;
  GSAP now drives the choreography. Random layout selection and fake proof do not
  serve the chosen direction.
- stitch-design-taste: durable semantic tokens and explicit component behavior;
  no Stitch connection is required or available for this repository.
- web-design-guidelines and React best practices: semantic controls, focus, reduced
  motion, image sizing and small client boundaries. Current primary guidelines:
  https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines.
- find-skills: current directory checked; official Anthropic and Vercel skills are
  already installed. Skill-installer then installed the five official GSAP skills:
  core, timeline, ScrollTrigger, React and performance, from
  https://github.com/greensock/gsap-skills. Runtime GSAP 3.15.1 and @gsap/react 2.1.2
  are locked in the repository. See MOTION.md for their application and captures.

Conflicting stylistic recipes are resolved through the user's chosen direction.
They are not layered indiscriminately. No third-party code, photos, logos, font
files, screenshots or video is copied into the production design.
