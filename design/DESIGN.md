## Audit and revision

The previous page used a generic serif hero, repeated feature/pricing cards,
unsubstantiated conversion statistics and unrelated web-development packages.
Keep the Next.js stack and existing domain; replace that content and visual system.
Source repository: https://github.com/Ilia01/services-site.

The initial forest/pear direction could resemble a generic dark software page.
The revised direction gives the hero to a tactile oak desk and the proof section
to an actual review artifact. No dashboard decoration, fake client proof, icon
grids or made-up performance counters. Photography is illustrative AI artwork.

Reference extraction: hero uses a 46:54 text/image composition; sample uses an
open intro beside a paper table; offer uses price opposite joinery and process;
contact pairs a short invitation with an oak macro and open FAQ rows.
No section is a repeated card grid. Nav stays 76px; hero actions fit small laptops.
Each section's generated reference is retained privately under work/design.

density 3 for breathing room. Mobile collapses into one readable column.
Only opacity/transform animate, and reduced motion disables the entrance.
Native details and a small keyboard-accessible tab component answer user actions.
