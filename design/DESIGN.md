## Audit of v1

The forest canvas, acid accent and generic furniture photo made the product feel
interchangeable. Repeated split layouts gave each section the same pace. The sample
was too visually small relative to the photography. Technical scores did not measure
that design failure. Keep the working data, scope, intake, framework and deployment.

## Design read

An editorial catalog-restoration service for furniture retailers and Shopify agencies.
The page should feel like a carefully prepared catalog proof: physical furniture,
source records, legible changes and an unmistakable blue editorial hand.

## Tokens

- Paper #F4F3EE: continuous substrate, explicitly light as selected by the user.
- Carbon #232622: text and structural rules.
- Cobalt #3450CB: actions, active state and actual review annotations.
- Blue wash #E5EAF8: a single scope-sheet surface.
- Pencil #666D65: secondary text with readable contrast.
- Sheet #FBFAF6: source and review paper, gently distinguished from the substrate.
- Instrument Sans: large 600-weight headings, 400/500 body and navigation.
- IBM Plex Mono: SKUs, file references, prices and functional sequencing only.
- Main width 1320px; gutters 48 desktop / 22 mobile; 100–140px chapter spacing.
- Display 108–132px desktop / 48–68px mobile, two explicit lines; body 17–18px.
- Rules are functional grid boundaries. Corners mostly square; buttons 3px.
- Layers: page 0, document 1, furniture 2, annotation 3, skip-link 10.

## Four distinct compositions

Hero: enormous two-line headline upper left, concise intro and CTA upper right.
Below, a broad furniture-and-source composition: our existing desk photograph is
clipped with native CSS to its silhouette; a real fictional supplier sheet sits
beside it; a blue price correction points to the exact SKU. No new raster asset or
generated mockup is needed. The image is illustrative, all price data is fictional.

Review: full-width masthead followed by a large open ledger. Data gets enough visual
space to be the proof. Two accessible tabs retain actual engine data and URL state.
Price changes use blue ink marks; retained values remain quiet. Actual report/input
links sit below the ledger. No fake window chrome or processing animation.

Offer: one flat scope sheet, enormous price, concise inclusions and a separate clear
controlled-import boundary. Follow with three open chronological process rows.

Contact: oversized invitation, email sample CTA and a small intake note. Questions
and answers use an open editorial layout so important intake details are visible.
Footer identifies the owner and leads back to the start.

## Motion and responsive behavior

The user's follow-up explicitly requests rich reference-informed animation.
GSAP choreographs the catalog assembly, masked chapter headings, drawn blue ink,
furniture parallax, desktop specimen pin, scope-sheet arrival and intake note.
Review states share a moving tab marker. See MOTION.md for precise ownership,
timings and lifecycle rules. Keep native scrolling and immediately usable actions.
OS reduced motion and the footer control remove the animated transforms, pinning,
smooth scrolling and transitions. Under 768px, the headline,
intro, source/object and correction stack into a readable sequence; no tiny scaled
whole-desktop scene. Verify 390 and 320px, including long SKUs and action labels.
